# Source title

- Source ID: unique identifier, checked against the source register.
- Origin and author: URL, repository path/commit, issue, or identified conversation.
- Source date and access date: actual dates; mark unknowns explicitly.
- Record type: original, authorized snapshot, translation, summary, or bibliographic record.
- Retrieval scope and limitations: what was actually read and what was not available.
- Handling: preserve after ingestion; add a separate dated correction instead of editing history.

## Safe source content

Use English. Label translations and summaries; do not pass them off as verbatim originals. Preserve provenance and unresolved statements. Prefer a bibliographic pointer over copying a copyrighted external work. Exclude secrets and personal data.

## Integration

Link the relevant topic pages, [source register](../sources/index.md), and [wiki index](../index.md). The register tracks later integrations; an immutable source record need not be rewritten each time its conclusions change. See [maintenance](../maintenance.md).
