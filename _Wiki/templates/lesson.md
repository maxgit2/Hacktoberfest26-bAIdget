# Problem or error signature

- Component/environment/version: actual affected context.
- Status: observed / workaround / verified fix; select the evidenced description.
- Evidence: source paths, safe error excerpt, issue or commit.

## Symptom and reproduction

Record a minimal reproduction and the exact recognizable failure. Use synthetic or redacted input.

## Cause and solution

Separate hypotheses from verified diagnosis. Explain the fix or workaround and its limits; include failed approaches only when they prevent repeated investigation.

## Verification and prevention

Record the exact command or steps, observed outcome, and remaining gaps. Link related technical pages and add this lesson to the [lessons register](../lessons/index.md), [wiki index](../index.md), and [log](../log.md). See [maintenance](../maintenance.md).
