# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

- Decision status: proposed / accepted / rejected / superseded; select one.
- Date: actual decision date.
- Authority and evidence: who decided, or explicitly say proposal; link a source/issue.
- Supersedes / superseded by: link only when applicable.

## Context and options

Describe the concrete constraint and relevant alternatives. Do not invent evaluations or approval.

## Choice and rationale

State the proposed or accepted choice, why it fits the current scope, and the assumptions.

## Consequences and follow-up

Describe affected contracts, tradeoffs, migration/reversal needs, and implementation/verification status. Link the topic pages and open questions. Add the finished record to the [decision register](../decisions/index.md), [wiki index](../index.md), and [log](../log.md). See [maintenance](../maintenance.md).
